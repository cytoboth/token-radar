Where the numbers come from

Every figure on this page is computed from that snapshot alone. No external call, no cached list, no hand-set multiple per protocol: one fit, applied to every row.

Launch odds

The share of protocols of the same age that already record a token, counted in the snapshot and smoothed by one to two so thin buckets cannot claim certainty. It is a cross-sectional rate, not a forecast, and it is shown beside the value rather than folded into it.

What was tested and rejected

The value curve was fitted with and without a per-category term and scored on held-out halves. The category term is only applied when it wins; the folds are printed below so the decision can be checked.
