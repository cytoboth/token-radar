Where the numbers come from

Every figure on this page is computed from the sealed snapshot alone. No external call, no cached list, no hand-set multiple per protocol: one robust fit, applied to every row.

Launch odds

The share of protocols of the same age that already record a token, fitted as a logistic curve on the snapshot's own records. It is a cross-sectional rate, not a forecast.

Tested and rejected

The value curve was fitted with and without a per-category term and scored on held-out halves. The category term is only applied when it wins; the folds are printed below so the decision can be checked.
